---
name: remember
description: Save a durable fact about the user to MEMORY.md
---

When the user tells you something to remember — a preference, a
decision, a commitment, or an outcome that changes their situation:

1. Write one line to MEMORY.md in this format:
   `- YYYY-MM-DD: <fact, with enough context to make sense later>`
   Use today's date.
2. Confirm back the single line you saved, in your own words.

Do not save credentials, tokens, codes, or card details. Do not save
a proposed action as if it were completed.
